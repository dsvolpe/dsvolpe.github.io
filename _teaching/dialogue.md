As part of UMD's Intergroup Dialogue initiative, I taught two semesters of the undergraduate course _Teaching and Learning about Cultural Diversity through Intergroup Dialogue; Equity and Inclusion in Engineering Design_ (2022 and 2024).

I also co-designed and co-taught one semester of a similar course for life sciences: _Navigating Diverse Social Environments through Intergroup Dialogue; Equity & Inclusion in the Life Sciences_ (2023).

Using my skills and knowledge gained from teaching these courses, I developed and taught a 4-session dialogue series for undergraduate students to explore the role of justice in physics (2022-2025). The schedule is below and I am happy to share more materials upon request.

I created this series in my role as Director for the [TREND undergraduate research program](/pm/trend/).
