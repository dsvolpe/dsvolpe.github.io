In 2020, I led a team of 4 to produce a video series highlighting the lives and work of 11 Latine physicists. We executed the project on a very modest budget ($10K) that I obtained through a grant proposal to APS (American Physical Society).
Project website: [rostros.umd.edu](https://www.rostros.umd.edu)
