- Program name: Terps in Space
- Funding: $277K
- Website: [terpsinspace.umd.edu](https://terpsinspace.umd.edu)
- Duration: 2016 - 2022
- My involvement: 2016 - 2022

Terps in Space was an undergraduate program where student teams desgined their own experiments to test the role of microgravity (conditions at the International Space Station) on a system of their choice. Team then wrote proposals and one of them was selected to fly their experiment to the ISS.

Some of my key contributions:
- Founded the program (as a community of the [SSEP](http://ssep.ncesse.org)) by securing initial and sustained [Maryland Space Grant Consortium](https://md.spacegrant.org) funding
- Designed and executed experiential program, recruiting and supporting ~5 teams of 2-5 undergraduate students (and their respective graduate student/faculty mentors) each semester
- Grew program from running it solo to directing a 3-member team by increasing yearly raised funds
- Expanded impact by establishing collaboration with local high schools to involve their students as program participants
- Co-designed and co-taught 6-module lecture series on microgravity experiment design and proposal writing
- Co-developed, co-implemented, and co-analyzed assessment surveys, leading to program improvements

Associated grants:
- Online Higher Ed Spaceflight Experiments Mentoring Program for Undergraduate and High School Students (PI)
- Implementation Plan for Student Spaceflight Experiments Program (SSEP) Mission 16 to the International Space Station (PI)
